## Local vs India-Wide Sources

### Local Sources (Quick Commerce)
- **Blinkit** - 10-30 minute delivery in metro cities
- Local warehouses
- Smaller sizes typically (500g, 1kg, 5kg)

### India-Wide Sources (E-Commerce)
- **Amazon, Flipkart, JioMart, BigBasket**
- Pan-India delivery
- All sizes available (500g to 5kg+)
- Longer delivery time (1-7 days)

### Wholesale Sources (B2B)
- **IndiaMART** - Business-to-business
- Bulk quantities (10kg, 25kg, 50kg)
- Better per-kg rates
- Minimum order quantities may apply

## Understanding the Data

### MRP (Maximum Retail Price)
- The printed price on product package
- Legal maximum seller can charge
- Reference for calculating discounts

### Sale Price (Current Price)
- What you actually pay
- After discounts/offers
- Can be lower than MRP

### Discount Percentage
- Calculated: `(MRP - Sale Price) / MRP × 100`
- Shows how much you save
- Higher percentage = better deal

### Per-Kg Rate
- For easy comparison across sizes
- 500g @ ₹145 = ₹290/kg
- 1kg @ ₹280 = ₹280/kg
- Bigger size often cheaper per kg

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Why am I seeing sample data?
**A:** You're on Streamlit Cloud which runs in demo mode. This shows how the system works with sample data. For real-time prices, deploy locally.

### Q: Are the product links real?
**A:** Yes! The links point to real search pages on each platform. Clicking will take you to that website to search for the product.

### Q: Why different brands for same product?
**A:** Each platform may stock different brands. We show what's typically available on each source.

### Q: Can I buy directly from the app?
**A:** No, but you can click the 🔗 Product Link to go to the source website and purchase there.

### Q: How often is data updated?
**A:** In demo mode, it's static sample data. In real-time mode (local deployment), prices are scraped daily.

### Q: What if I need more sizes?
**A:** The system can show any size variant found during scraping. Demo shows common sizes (500g, 1kg, 5kg, 10kg, 25kg).

### Q: Can I export this data?
**A:** Yes! In the full version, there's an export to Excel button for the complete price comparison.
